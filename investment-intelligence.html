The Committee Problem

Committee decisions deserve evidence.
Most arrive with none.

Investment committees govern the largest capital decisions an institution makes. Yet the research feeding those decisions is too often built on assertion, not evidence. Research is assembled from analyst reports, PDFs, emails, spreadsheets, terminal exports, and regulator publications — scattered across systems that do not talk to each other. Committee members spend hours validating evidence that should already be linked to its source.

Research Without Proof

Research notes lack evidence.

Analyst notes summarize, assert, and recommend — but rarely link claims back to the official source, page, or paragraph they came from. Committees are asked to trust conclusions they cannot verify.

Intuition Over Evidence

Committee decisions on intuition.

When evidence is missing, decisions default to seniority, conviction, and narrative. The committee record reflects who argued what — not what the verified information actually supported.

Preparation Drag

Preparation takes days, not hours.

Investment teams spend too much time collecting, reconciling, and validating company information before research can begin.

Decision Accountability

Decisions cannot be reconstructed.

When decisions are challenged, committees cannot reconstruct why they were made. Governance reviews become dependent on memory, emails, and scattered documents — not on a replayable record of the evidence that was considered at the time.

How ROUA Solves This
Evidence → Reasoning → Committee Output
Replayable decision records with complete evidence lineage — every claim, source, and reasoning path preserved for governance review.

The consequence is consistent across institutions: decisions become difficult to verify, difficult to audit, and difficult to defend.
