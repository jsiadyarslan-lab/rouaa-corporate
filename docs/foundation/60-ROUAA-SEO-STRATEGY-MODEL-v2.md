# 0. Purpose

This document defines the long-term search strategy for ROUAA.

It does **not** define keywords only.

It defines how ROUAA becomes the reference authority in its category.

SEO is not a traffic strategy.

SEO is a market-positioning strategy.

---

# 1. The Strategic Objective

The objective is **not**:

* Rank for "stock news"
* Rank for "trading signals"
* Rank for "AI trading"

Those are crowded, low-intent, and inconsistent with ROUAA's positioning.

The objective is:

# Own the Financial Intelligence category.

ROUAA should become the reference platform whenever institutions search for:

* Financial Intelligence
* Decision Intelligence
* Evidence-based financial analysis
* Institutional market intelligence
* Financial knowledge infrastructure

---

Traditional financial websites compete inside existing categories.

Examples:

```text

Market News

```

```text

```

# 3. Search Philosophy

Search traffic is divided into five layers.

```text
Discovery

↓

Understanding

↓

Evaluation

↓

Comparison

↓

Enterprise Intent
```

ROUAA must create content for every layer.

---

# Layer 1 — Discovery

Audience:

People discovering a problem.

Searches:

* What is financial intelligence?
* Difference between market data and intelligence
* Evidence-based analysis
* Financial knowledge graph

Goal:

Teach.

Not sell.

---

# Layer 2 — Understanding

Audience:

Professionals exploring solutions.

Searches:

* Financial intelligence platform
* Investment intelligence software
* Risk intelligence
* Financial AI platform

Goal:

Explain methodology.

---

# Layer 3 — Evaluation

Audience:

Institutional buyers.

Searches:

* Bloomberg alternatives
* Refinitiv alternatives
* Financial intelligence platform for banks
* Enterprise research platform

Goal:

Demonstrate differentiation.

---

# Layer 4 — Comparison

Audience:

Decision makers.

Content:

* Platform comparisons
* Architecture comparisons
* Cost of fragmented workflows
* Evidence vs black-box AI

Goal:

Enable procurement decisions.

---

# Layer 5 — Enterprise Intent

Audience:

Qualified institutional buyers.

Content:

* Security
* Governance
* APIs
* Deployment
* White Label
* Enterprise architecture

Goal:

Generate meetings.

# 18. The Missing Piece

One structural gap still remains after rebuilding documents 13, 23, 24, 58, 59 and 60.

The website architecture now correctly explains the ecosystem, **but it still treats products as isolated catalogue entries**.

Your Product Portfolio actually contains **multiple product lines that together form complete enterprise offerings**. For example:

* A media organisation doesn't buy only **Financial News Engine**; it often buys a newsroom stack (News Engine + Agency Agent + Reports + Video + Infographics + Audio).
* A broker doesn't buy only **Trading Intelligence Dashboard**; it buys a trading intelligence suite.
* An asset manager buys a research and decision workflow, not a single report generator.

That means there is one more document that should exist between **Platform** and **Products**:

```text
Platform

↓

Solution Suites

↓

Products

↓

Components
```

Without this layer, sales, website navigation, pricing, implementation, and enterprise conversations all have to reconstruct the same grouping repeatedly.

I would add a new foundational document:

**63-ROUAA-SOLUTION-SUITES-MODEL-v1.md**

This document would define the commercial solution suites (Media Suite, Trading Suite, Research Suite, Risk Suite, Developer Suite, etc.) and become the bridge between the ecosystem architecture and the individual product catalogue. In retrospect, this is a missing architectural document, not just a missing website page.
