Institutions do not buy isolated AI capabilities. They need intelligence systems that close specific gaps in research, markets, risk, media, and trading — from verified financial information to decisions and outputs they can defend.

Solution 01 — Banks · Financial Institutions · Market Teams

Understand What Moved the Market

ROUA Product: Market Intelligence

Connect market movements to verified financial events, official sources, and structured market context — so teams can understand not only what moved, but why.

For institutions that need to understand why markets moved.
Problem
Market movements appear without context. Teams see the move but cannot quickly identify which verified economic, financial, regulatory, or corporate event drove it — or whether the move is information-driven or noise.
ROUA Approach
Connects market movements to verified financial events, official sources, and structured market-impact context. Capabilities: Economic & Market Event Intelligence · Market Impact Analysis · Macro & Sector Intelligence · Scenario & Outlook Intelligence.
Outcome
Teams understand market moves with verified evidence — not assumptions. Structured market context replaces fragmented information gathering.
Solution 02 — Investment Firms · Asset Managers · Research Teams

Defensible Investment Decisions

ROUA Product: Investment Intelligence

For CIO offices and investment teams — portfolio managers, research, and investment committees that reject analysis without provenance.

For investment committees that reject analysis without provenance.
Problem
Investment committees reject analysis without provenance. Decisions made on intuition don't survive governance review. Research notes that say "we believe" lose to notes that say "the document shows."
ROUA Approach
Every fact in every research note links back to its source document, page, and paragraph — Governance rules, validation, and confidence scoring operate before analysis reaches committee.
Outcome
Investment decisions arrive at committee with full evidence chains — governance-ready, evidence-linked, defensible. The conversation shifts from "is this right?" to "what do we do about what the documents show?"
Solution 03 — Trading Desks · Quant Teams · Broker-Dealers

Turn Market Intelligence Into Trading Decisions

ROUA Product: Trading Intelligence

Combine evaluated signals, smart chart intelligence, market scanning, predictive-market context, and controlled automated execution in one trading intelligence workflow.

For trading teams that need evaluated, evidence-aware workflows.
Problem
Traders work across fragmented tools — charts, scanners, signals, news, and execution systems — without a unified intelligence layer connecting verified market context to signal evaluation and controlled execution.
ROUA Approach
Combines multi-model signal evaluation (Signal Council), smart chart intelligence, advanced market scanning, predictive-market context, and controlled automated execution — connecting verified financial intelligence to governed trading workflows. ROUA does not custody assets; it operates as an intelligence and orchestration layer above connected execution systems.
Outcome
Traders move from fragmented market signals to evaluated, evidence-aware trading workflows with controlled execution — and an auditable record of the decision context behind every action.
Solution 04 — Financial Media · News Agencies · Research Publishers · Digital Platforms

Produce Verifiable Financial Intelligence

ROUA Product: Media Intelligence

From verified financial events to analyzed news, research reports, strategic briefings, video, infographics, and publishable intelligence — with editorial control preserved.

For media organizations that produce financial intelligence.
Problem
Media organizations must publish faster without sacrificing accuracy. Every claim must be verifiable. Every source must be trusted. News flows faster than editorial teams can verify.
ROUA Approach
Transforms verified financial events into analyzed news, reports, video scripts, infographics, and editorial feeds — with an independent publishing agent and white-label distribution. Evidence is the foundation; editorial teams retain review and publication control.
Outcome
Media organizations produce more intelligence products — news, reports, video, infographics — with every claim traceable to an official source. Editorial time shifts from gathering to interpreting.
Solution 05 — Risk · Compliance · Asset Owners

Monitor Risk Before It Becomes Exposure

ROUA Product: Risk Intelligence

For risk teams that need evidence-backed monitoring.
Problem
Risk teams face fragmented regulatory, geopolitical, financial, and market information with limited ability to connect events to affected exposures, counterparties, and jurisdictions.
ROUA Approach
Connects official regulatory and financial events to evidence, entities, exposures, and monitored risk contexts. Capabilities: Regulatory Monitoring · Exposure Analysis · Compliance Evidence Packs · Risk Scenario Intelligence — with provenance throughout the intelligence chain.
Outcome
Risk teams receive structured, evidence-backed intelligence they can investigate, escalate, and reconstruct during review.
