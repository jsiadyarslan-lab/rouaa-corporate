What This Is Not

ROUA is evidence infrastructure producing intelligence objects — consumed by institutional decision environments. It is not a trading terminal, not a Bloomberg clone, not an AI chatbot, and not a stock recommendation engine. The experiences below show how verified intelligence flows into research, risk, market, media, and developer workflows — not a UI to browse tickers or chat with an assistant.
