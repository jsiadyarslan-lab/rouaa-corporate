Market Intelligence Infrastructure
The intelligence layer behind every market move.

Turn market events into decision-grade intelligence — with every price move connected to the verified event that caused it, the source it came from, and the reasoning path that explains it. ROUAA is not a market data feed. It is the intelligence infrastructure behind market decisions.

Market Data vs. Market Intelligence

Market data platforms tell you what happened. ROUAA explains why it happened, what evidence supports it, how it affects portfolios, and preserves the complete reasoning chain for future review.

ROUAA does not replace Bloomberg, FactSet, or Refinitiv. It sits above them — consuming their data alongside official sources and producing governance-ready intelligence that your desk, compliance, and audit teams can all trust.

The Market Problem

Charts show where price moved.
They never show why.

Trading desks, quant teams, research teams, and brokers operate on market signals that describe movement — but rarely explain the verified events and facts that caused it. Market decisions are reviewed long after execution. Without evidence, firms cannot explain why a position was taken, what information justified it, or whether the decision was consistent with policy.

Context Loss

Price moves without explanations.

Trading desks see the move, but cannot reconstruct why it happened — which event, which source, which fact drove the price. Without context intelligence, decisions are made on price alone, not on the verified information that moved it.

Price Without Cause

Charts show where, not why.

A chart tells you a security moved 4% in twenty minutes. It does not tell you which earnings release, regulatory filing, or geopolitical event drove the move — or whether the move was information-driven or noise.

Signals Without Context

Indicators without event context.

RSI, MACD, and momentum indicators fire without knowing whether a verified event justifies the signal. Indicators describe state — they do not connect price action to the underlying facts that should govern a trading decision.

Decisions Without Proof

No evidence chain for trades.

When a trade is reviewed, the rationale lives in chat messages and trader memory — not in a reconstructable chain linking decision, signal, event, and source. Compliance, risk, and audit are left to rebuild what should never have been lost.

Decision Accountability

Trading decisions cannot be reconstructed.

When a position is reviewed weeks later, the event, evidence, portfolio exposure, reasoning, and execution context that justified the trade cannot be reconstructed. Governance reviews depend on memory and chat logs, not on a replayable record of what was known at the time.

ROUAA
Every trading decision preserved with complete lineage — event, evidence, portfolio exposure, reasoning, and execution context reconstructable on demand for compliance, risk, and audit.

The consequence is consistent across institutions: market decisions become difficult to verify, difficult to audit, and difficult to defend.

Capabilities

Four capabilities. One evidence architecture.

Each capability transforms verified market events into evidence-backed trading intelligence — every output inherits the same evidence layer.

01 · Event Impact Analysis

Event impact, quantified.

Connect verified market events — earnings, filings, macro releases, geopolitical developments — to instrument-level impact, with every impact assessment traceable to the source document, page, and paragraph.

02 · Trading Context

Context for every signal.

Surround every price move and indicator with the verified events and facts that explain it — so traders act on understanding, not on pattern. The why ships with the what.

03 · Portfolio Intelligence

Portfolio-aware intelligence.

Map market events to holdings, exposures, and positions — surfacing the instruments most affected by a verified event, with exposure links preserved in the evidence layer.

04 · Scenario Intelligence

Scenarios, not signals.

Construct defensible trading scenarios from verified events and facts — each branch carries probability, assumptions, supporting evidence, and alternative scenarios. Base, optimistic, and pessimistic paths articulated with confidence scoring and evidence packages attached.
