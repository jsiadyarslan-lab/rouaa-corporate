What ROUAA Is — and Is Not

لإزالة أي التباس

ROUAA is not
✕ A trading signal provider
✕ A replacement for Bloomberg or FactSet
✕ A black-box AI analyst
✕ A data vendor
✕ A ChatGPT wrapper for finance
ROUAA is
✓ Intelligence infrastructure layer
✓ Evidence and reasoning system
✓ Decision operating layer
✓ Audit-ready knowledge platform
✓ The connective tissue between data and decisions
ROUAA does not replace market terminals. It adds verified intelligence, institutional evidence, and explainable reasoning — on top of your existing stack.
