Product Briefing Request

Request a product briefing.

Every briefing follows a three-stage process: Product fit → Workflow review → Deployment discussion. You will leave knowing whether ROUA's intelligence products fit your institution — not with a sales pitch.

What A Briefing Can Cover

Four conversations a briefing can include — none overclaim.

You choose what matters. The briefing is structured around your questions — not a product walkthrough. ROUA does not promise outcomes during a briefing; it demonstrates how the products work and what they can — and cannot — support.

Product Fit

Which ROUA intelligence products — Investment, Market, Risk, Media, or Developer — map to your institution's decisions, and which do not. Honest scope, not a forced fit.

Evidence Requirements

Source hierarchy, evidence chain structure, confidence scoring, and how ROUA handles sources your institution cares about — central banks, regulators, exchanges, filings.

Deployment Model

Platform access, API integration, or private deployment — with the trade-offs of each, and what your governance, security, and operational teams will need to review.

Institutional Workflow

How ROUA products fit inside your existing investment, risk, editorial, or research workflow — and where the handoff points sit between ROUA output and your downstream decisions.

What To Expect

Three stages, from first contact to deployment discussion.

Stage 01 — Product Fit

A 30-minute call to map your institution's decisions against ROUA's five intelligence products. Outcome: a written note on which products fit, which do not, and why — sent within two business days.

Stage 02 — Workflow Review

A 60-minute structured walkthrough of the evidence chain, source hierarchy, and sample outputs — mapped to your workflow. Outcome: you see exactly how a ROUA output is constructed and what it would replace in your current process.

Stage 03 — Deployment Discussion

A conversation with your technology, governance, and procurement teams on deployment model, integration, security review, and commercial structure. Outcome: a deployment plan scoped to your institution — or a clear no-fit decision.

A briefing is not a sales demo. If ROUA's products are not the right fit for your institution, we will tell you during Stage 01 — and recommend what would be.

Briefing Request

Six fields. Two minutes.

A ROUA institutional lead will respond within one business day to schedule Stage 01 (Product Fit). We do not share your information, and we do not add you to marketing sequences.
