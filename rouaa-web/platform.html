Platform Adoption Workflow

How ROUA lives inside your institution.

ROUA does not replace your data warehouse, your OMS, your research tools, or your editorial CMS. It is deployed alongside them as an intelligence infrastructure layer — and your existing systems receive evidence-linked intelligence through the integration surface.

01
Your Existing Stack
Your data warehouse, research tools, risk engine, OMS, editorial CMS, and internal portals stay exactly where they are. ROUA does not replace them — it sits alongside them as an intelligence layer.
↓
02
ROUA Deployed Alongside
The ROUA intelligence infrastructure is deployed in the environment that fits your governance — Cloud SaaS, Private Cloud, On-Premise inside your data center, or Hybrid. Same architecture, same evidence model, your boundary.
↓
03
Integration Surface
Your existing services call ROUA through the integration surface — REST API for on-demand queries, streaming for event-driven workflows, SDK for typed access. ROUA is called from your systems; it does not sit in front of them.
↓
04
Evidence-Linked Intelligence Delivered In
Intelligence outputs flow into your existing systems — research briefs into your research portal, risk alerts into your risk engine, editorial drafts into your CMS. Each output carries its evidence chain: source document, page, paragraph, provenance. Your systems own the user experience; ROUA supplies the evidence-linked intelligence.
↓
05
Governed Decisions, Your Authority
Your teams make the decisions — ROUA provides the evidence layer. Every material claim in every output can be reconstructed back to its official source, weeks or months later, for committee review, audit, or regulatory inquiry. The institution retains decision authority; ROUA provides the defensible intelligence layer underneath.

One platform · deployed alongside your stack · intelligence delivered into your existing systems · your teams retain decision authority.
