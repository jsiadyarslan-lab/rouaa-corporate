The Trading Desk Problem

Trading desks have more tools than ever.
Less decision certainty when speed matters most.

Fragmented Intelligence

Market data, research, events, and execution systems exist separately. Traders spend time connecting information instead of making decisions.

Slow Decision Cycles

Missing Context

Market movements show what happened — but trading decisions often lack the verified financial and event context needed to understand why.

No Governance

Automation without governance creates operational and decision risk. Institutions need controlled execution workflows that preserve approval, decision context, and audit records — not just speed.

The Market & Trading Intelligence Stack

From market context to governed trading action.

ROUA combines the core intelligence and execution-support capabilities required to move from market observation to governed trading action — without forcing institutions to replace their existing execution infrastructure.

01 · Signal Council

10-Model Signal Council

A 10-model intelligence council independently evaluates a trading signal across defined criteria, then aggregates model assessments into a governed consensus on signal quality and strength before the signal reaches the trader.

Consensus represents agreement among evaluation models — not a guarantee of market outcome.
02 · Smart Chart Intelligence

Unified analytical workspace

A unified analytical workspace combines technical indicators, price structure, multi-timeframe analysis, event context, and market intelligence so traders can evaluate the chart without switching between disconnected analytical tools.

03 · Advanced Market Scanner

Surface candidates for deeper evaluation

Scan instruments and markets across technical conditions, price structure, volatility, momentum, events, and configurable criteria — surfacing candidates for deeper chart and signal evaluation.

04 · Predictive Markets

Market-implied expectations & scenarios

Market-based probability and expectation signals that provide an additional context layer alongside price, technical structure, and verified financial intelligence.

05 · Automated Execution

Controlled Automated Execution

Route approved trading workflows through three specialized automated executors designed for defined trading timeframes, with configurable controls, monitoring, and an auditable execution record.
