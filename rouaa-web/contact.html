Institutional Briefing Request

Request an institutional briefing.

Every briefing follows a structured five-stage process — from institutional assessment to deployment planning. The objective is a clear fit assessment: what ROUA can support, what it cannot, and what deployment would require.

What A Briefing Can Cover

Four conversations a briefing can cover.

You choose what matters. The briefing is structured around your institutional questions, workflow, evidence requirements, and deployment constraints — not a generic product walkthrough.

Product Fit

Which ROUA intelligence products — Investment, Market, Risk, Media, Trading, or Developer — map to your institution's decisions, and which do not. Honest scope, not a forced fit.

Evidence Requirements

Source hierarchy, evidence chain structure, confidence scoring, and how ROUA handles sources your institution cares about — central banks, regulators, statistical agencies, exchanges, issuers, filings, and other authoritative sources.

Deployment Model

Platform access, API integration, or private deployment — with the trade-offs of each, and what your governance, security, and operational teams will need to review.

Institutional Workflow

How ROUA products fit inside your existing investment, risk, editorial, or research workflow — and where the handoff points sit between ROUA output and your downstream decisions.

What To Expect

Five stages, from first contact to deployment planning.

Stage 01 — Institutional Assessment

A 30-minute call to map your institution’s decisions against ROUA’s intelligence products. Outcome: a clear assessment of which products fit, which do not, and why.

Stage 02 — Source & Workflow Mapping

We map the official sources your institution relies on — central banks, regulators, exchanges, issuers — against your research, risk, editorial, or trading workflow. Outcome: a source-to-decision map.

Stage 03 — Workflow Demonstration

A 60-minute structured walkthrough of the evidence chain and sample outputs — mapped to your workflow. Outcome: you see how a ROUA output is constructed, where it fits, and which workflow steps it could augment or replace.

Stage 04 — Pilot Definition

If fit is confirmed, we scope a pilot against a defined institutional workflow — success criteria, evidence requirements, governance controls, and your evaluation framework. Outcome: a pilot plan or a clear no-go.

Stage 05 — Deployment Planning

A conversation with your technology, governance, and procurement teams on deployment model, integration, security, data governance, and commercial structure. Outcome: a deployment plan scoped to your institution — or a clear no-fit decision.

If ROUA's products are not the right fit, we will tell you during Stage 01 — and recommend what would be.
