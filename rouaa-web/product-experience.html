Product Experience Center

One governed intelligence object.
Four decision environments. One integration layer.

ROUA is not a product suite. It is evidence infrastructure that produces governed, evidence-linked Intelligence Objects — consumed by four decision environments (Investment, Market & Trading, Risk, Media) and one integration layer (Developer Platform). The same FOMC Rate Decision feeds an investment brief, a market impact context, a risk exposure analysis, a media article, and a developer API event — same truth, different decisions, all defensible.

What This Is Not

ROUA is not another destination terminal. It is an intelligence infrastructure layer that produces governed, evidence-linked outputs for institutional workflows. The experiences below show how the same intelligence object is consumed differently across investment, market & trading, risk, media, and developer workflows.

One Object, Four Environments + One Integration Layer
