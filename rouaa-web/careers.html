Careers at ROUA

Build intelligence products
that institutions can defend.

We are building the team behind ROUA. We welcome expressions of interest from engineers, researchers, and institutional practitioners who want to build intelligence products and the evidence infrastructure beneath them. If you want to work on difficult problems at the intersection of financial intelligence, evidence infrastructure, AI governance, and institutional software — and take meaningful ownership of systems that must earn trust — we want to hear from you.

Areas of Work Our Culture
Why People Join ROUA

Three reasons people join ROUA.

Work That Has Weight

You are not optimizing ad clicks. You are building intelligence infrastructure for institutions whose decisions can carry significant financial, regulatory, and reputational consequences. The work has weight because the systems must be trusted.

Hard Problems, No Off-The-Shelf Answers

Evidence architecture. Knowledge representation. Governed AI on governed inputs. Source hierarchy design. These are problems without simple off-the-shelf answers — and you will help shape the systems rather than simply maintain someone else's solution.

Compounding Work, Long Horizons

ROUA builds products and infrastructure that take years to mature. We do not optimize for quarterly feature releases. We optimize for durable institutional trust rather than short-lived feature velocity. Your work is expected to compound across systems, methodologies, and products.

Culture

Six principles that shape how we work.

Principle 01

Provenance In Everything

If you make a material claim — in code, design, research, or a meeting — be ready to show the evidence behind it. We distinguish clearly between what we know, what we infer, and what we still need to verify.

Principle 02

Governance Before Output

We do not ship what we cannot defend. Material changes are reviewed before they become institutional outputs — through the appropriate engineering, design, research, or methodology review.

Principle 03

Institutional Outcomes, Human Workflows

We build for institutional outcomes through the people who use the system every day — analysts, researchers, risk teams, editors, traders, engineers, and decision-makers. The institution is the buyer; the workflow is where value is delivered.

Principle 04

Open Methodology

We publish the methodologies and principles that institutions should be able to examine. Proprietary implementation and security-sensitive details remain protected where necessary.

Principle 05

Long Horizons

We hire for long-term contribution. We want people who can grow with the problems, take increasing ownership, and help shape the company as the systems mature.

Principle 06

Dissent Is Welcome

Building intelligence products that institutions can defend requires that we get the architecture right. That requires disagreement. We hire people who will tell us when we are wrong — and we listen.

Areas of Work

Where We Work

We hire across several core disciplines as the company grows. Strong candidates can start conversations even when a specific role is not publicly posted. If your work fits one of these areas, send us a note.

Area 01

Engineering

Build the systems that ingest, process, govern, and deliver financial intelligence — from source acquisition through evidence and intelligence workflows. You will own systems that institutions depend on — and the evidence layer beneath them.

Our stack includes NestJS, Python/FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Redis, and related data infrastructure.

Typical profiles: senior backend, senior data, platform, frontend engineering.

Area 02

Intelligence

AI/ML engineers, NLP specialists, knowledge graph engineers, and information scientists. Building governed AI on governed evidence — not wrappers around foundation models. You will work where AI meets auditability.

Typical profiles: senior ML, NLP research, knowledge graph, information science.

Area 03

Research

Methodology researchers, financial economists, information architects, and visiting researchers. Driving the open methodology that defines how ROUA verifies sources and governs output. You will help shape and publish the methodologies and principles behind the system.

Typical profiles: senior methodology research, financial economics, information architecture, visiting research.

Area 04

Operations

Source operations, intelligence operations, compliance, and program management. The people who keep the registry monitored, the pipeline healthy, and the company aligned with its institutional standards.

Typical profiles: source operations, intelligence operations, compliance, program management.

Area 05

Institutional Engagement

Institutional engagement, partnerships, enterprise deployment, and strategy. People who engage with CIO offices, research heads, and editors-in-chief — not SaaS salespeople. You will represent ROUA to institutions that hold us to high standards.

Typical profiles: institutional engagement, partnerships, enterprise deployment, strategy.

Expression of Interest

What You Will Own

Ownership, not tickets.

ROUA is an early-stage company. Many of these disciplines will begin as individual ownership areas and expand as the company grows. If you are excited by building from first principles rather than maintaining an existing system, this is the right stage to join.

Build From First Principles

You will work on systems where architecture, methodology, and product behavior are still being shaped.

Own Outcomes

Roles are structured around ownership of systems and workflows, not isolated tickets.

Work Across Disciplines

Financial intelligence requires engineers, researchers, domain specialists, and product thinkers to work together.

Challenge the System

Strong candidates are expected to question assumptions, surface failure modes, and improve the architecture.

How We Hire

A four-stage process, designed to be two-way.

We hire deliberately. The process is designed as much for you to evaluate us as for us to evaluate you. Every stage is a conversation — not a test.

01
Initial Conversation
A 45-minute call with the hiring manager. We discuss the role, your trajectory, and what you are looking for. No technical tests at this stage.
↓
02
Deep Dive
A 90-minute conversation with two team members. For engineering: a system design discussion. For research: a methodology discussion. For business: a workflow discussion.
↓
03
Work Sample
A paid work sample — a real problem relevant to the role, scoped to a reasonable time commitment. We pay for your time. You see how we work. We see how you think.
↓
04
Team Conversation
A 60-minute conversation with two future colleagues. Cultural fit, working style, and your questions for us. We want you to leave knowing whether ROUA is the right place for you.

Send us a note.

Tell us what you work on, what you would want to build at ROUA, and why. We review expressions of interest on an ongoing basis and respond when there is a meaningful fit.
