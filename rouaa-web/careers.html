Why Work At ROUAA

Three reasons people join ROUAA.

Mission That Matters

You are not optimizing ad clicks. You are building infrastructure that institutions use to defend decisions worth billions — to committees, regulators, and the public. The work has weight.

Hard Technical Problems

Evidence architecture. Knowledge graphs at institutional scale. Governed AI on verified inputs. Source hierarchy design. These are problems without off-the-shelf answers — and you will work on them.

Long Time Horizons

ROUAA builds infrastructure that takes years to mature. We do not optimize for quarterly feature releases. We optimize for institutional trust over decades. Your work compounds.

Culture

Six principles that shape how we work.

Principle 01

Provenance In Everything

If you make a claim — in code, in design, in a meeting — be ready to show the source. We don't accept "I think." We accept "the document shows."

Principle 02

Governance Before Output

We do not ship what we cannot defend. Code review, design review, methodology review — all happen before publication, not after.

Principle 03

Institutional, Not Individual

We build for institutions, not for users. The buyer is the CIO office, the research head, the compliance lead. We optimize for institutional outcomes, not individual screen time.

Principle 04

Open Methodology

We publish how we work. The Research Institute exists to make our methodology public. If we are not willing to publish it, we should not be doing it.

Principle 05

Long Horizons

We hire for careers, not for gigs. We expect people to grow with the company over years — and we structure roles, compensation, and growth paths accordingly.

Principle 06

Dissent Is Welcome

Building trust infrastructure requires that we get the architecture right. That requires disagreement. We hire people who will tell us when we are wrong — and we listen.

Open Roles

Five functions. Multiple roles.

ROUAA hires across five functions. Specific open roles change as we scale — but the function structure stays stable. If your function fits, we want to hear from you even if no specific role is listed.

Function 01

Engineering

Backend, frontend, data infrastructure, and platform engineers. NestJS, Python/FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Redis. Building the 7-layer architecture in production.

Sample roles: Senior Backend Engineer · Senior Data Engineer · Platform Engineer · Frontend Engineer

Function 02

Intelligence

AI/ML engineers, NLP specialists, knowledge graph engineers, and information scientists. Building governed AI on verified evidence — not wrappers around foundation models.

Sample roles: Senior ML Engineer · NLP Research Engineer · Knowledge Graph Engineer · Information Scientist

Function 03

Research

Methodology researchers, financial economists, information architects, and visiting researchers. Driving the open methodology that defines how ROUAA works.

Sample roles: Senior Methodology Researcher · Financial Economist · Information Architect · Visiting Researcher

Function 04

Operations

Source operations, intelligence operations, compliance, and program management. The people who keep the registry monitored, the pipeline healthy, and the company aligned.

Sample roles: Source Operations Lead · Intelligence Operations Manager · Compliance Manager · Program Manager

Function 05

Business

Institutional engagement, partnerships, enterprise deployment, and strategy. People who engage with CIO offices, research heads, and editors-in-chief — not SaaS salespeople.

Sample roles: Institutional Engagement Lead · Partnerships Manager · Enterprise Deployment Manager · Strategy Lead

How We Hire

A four-stage process, designed to be two-way.

We hire slowly. The process is designed as much for you to evaluate us as for us to evaluate you. Every stage is a conversation — not a test.

01
Initial Conversation
A 45-minute call with the hiring manager. We discuss the role, your trajectory, and what you are looking for. No technical tests at this stage.
↓
02
Deep Dive
A 90-minute conversation with two team members. For engineering: a system design discussion. For research: a methodology discussion. For business: a workflow discussion.
↓
03
Work Sample
A paid work sample — a real problem relevant to the role, scoped to a reasonable time commitment. We pay for your time. You see how we work. We see how you think.
↓
04
Team Conversation
A 60-minute conversation with two future colleagues. Cultural fit, working style, and your questions for us. We want you to leave knowing whether ROUAA is the right place for you.
