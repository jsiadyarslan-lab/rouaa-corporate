Source Lifecycle

How sources enter and remain in the registry.

Every source in the registry follows a governed lifecycle — from discovery to retirement. This is what ensures 411+ sources remain trusted, monitored, and current.

Discovery
Source identified through institutional request or landscape monitoring
↓
Verification
Authority confirmed · official status validated
↓
Classification
Trust tier assigned · type and jurisdiction categorized
↓
Monitoring
Continuous monitoring · ingestion adapters configured
↓
Health Tracking
Source health monitored · warnings and alerts tracked
↓
Retirement
Deprecated sources removed · evidence chains preserved
