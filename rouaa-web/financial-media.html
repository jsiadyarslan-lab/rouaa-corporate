The Media Problem

Speed and accuracy used to be a trade-off.
ROUAA removes the trade-off.

The Speed Problem

News flows faster than editorial teams can verify. By the time a story is checked, the next story has already broken. Newsrooms fall behind.

The Accuracy Problem

Every claim must be verifiable. Every source must be trusted. Aggregated feeds and AI wrappers cannot prove where their facts come from.

The Defensibility Problem

Reuters-grade defensibility requires every published claim to trace back to an official source. Most platforms cannot meet this standard at speed.
