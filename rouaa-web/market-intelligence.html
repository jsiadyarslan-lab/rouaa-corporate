The Market Problem

Markets move on events.
Institutions need to understand why.

Trading desks, research teams, banks, brokers, and financial institutions monitor economic releases, central-bank decisions, regulatory actions, corporate disclosures, and market developments across fragmented sources. The challenge is not access to information — it is turning source-linked events into structured market understanding quickly enough to support decisions.

Context Loss

Market events without context.

Institutions see individual events, releases, and market moves without a connected view of what changed, why it matters, and which markets or sectors may be affected.

Price Without Cause

Events without market impact.

A market move can appear without the event context behind it. ROUA connects material price and market developments to relevant earnings releases, regulatory actions, economic events, and other source-linked information — helping institutions distinguish documented drivers from unverified interpretation.

Market Moves Without Context

Market signals without event context.

Price, volume, volatility, and technical indicators describe market conditions, but they do not by themselves explain which source-linked economic, financial, regulatory, or corporate events may be driving those conditions. ROUA connects market developments to the underlying events and evidence that provide institutional context.

Decisions Without Proof

Market analysis without provenance.

When a market assessment is reviewed, the rationale may live across research notes, chat messages, analyst memory, and disconnected systems — not in a reconstructable chain linking assessment, event, evidence, and source. Compliance, risk, and audit are left to rebuild what should never have been lost.

Decision Accountability

Decisions without a replayable evidence trail.

When an institutional assessment is reviewed weeks later, the event, evidence, affected exposure, reasoning, and decision context that informed it may be difficult to reconstruct. Governance reviews depend on fragmented records rather than a replayable account of what was known at the time.

ROUA
Each market assessment preserves its source, evidence, reasoning, and relevant context for institutional review.

The consequence is consistent across institutions: market decisions become difficult to verify, difficult to audit, and difficult to defend.

Capabilities

Four capabilities. One evidence architecture.

Each capability transforms source-linked market events into evidence-backed market intelligence — each output inherits the same evidence layer.

01 · Economic & Market Event Intelligence

Understand what changed.

Track economic releases, central-bank decisions, regulatory actions, corporate disclosures, and other material financial events from source-linked sources — with the facts and evidence behind each event preserved.

02 · Market Impact Analysis

Understand what it affects.

Connect source-linked events to affected markets, sectors, instruments, and financial themes to provide structured market-impact context rather than isolated headlines.

03 · Macro & Sector Intelligence

Connect events across markets.

Relate macroeconomic developments, monetary policy, sectors, commodities, currencies, and other market relationships into a connected intelligence view.

04 · Scenario & Outlook Intelligence

Assess what may follow.

Build evidence-backed scenarios from source-linked events, financial facts, and explicit assumptions — with alternative paths clearly separated from observed information. Scenarios support institutional assessment; they are not presented as predictions or guaranteed outcomes.

How It Works

From event detection to institutional decision.

The buyer workflow — not the internal pipeline. Where ROUA enters the institution's market intelligence operation, and what changes at each step.

01
Pre-Session Monitoring Before the market opens
Institutional teams start the day with source-linked event context — central bank decisions, economic releases, regulatory actions, and corporate disclosures detected through configured source monitoring, with the evidence behind each event preserved from the moment of detection.
↓
02
Event Detection & Verification When an event breaks
A material event arrives not as a headline but as a structured intelligence object — the verified fact, the official source document, and the provenance trail (statement, paragraph, exact text) preserved together. The team starts from “what does this mean,” not “what happened.”
↓
03
Impact Assessment Connecting event to exposure
The event is placed into institutional context — affected instruments, sectors, macro relationships, and relevant portfolio exposure become one connected intelligence view. Relationships between the official event and market exposure surface that isolated data alone cannot reveal.
↓
04
Trading / Research Decision Acting with evidence
The decision — trading, research, or risk — is made with the evidence chain attached. Scenario branches carry explicit assumptions clearly separated from source-linked facts. Each decision output inherits the same evidence layer.
↓
05
Post-Decision Review Audit & governance
Weeks or months later, the market assessment is reconstructable — the event, the evidence, the impact assessment, the reasoning path, and the decision context that justified the call at the time. Governance reviews depend on replayable records, not fragmented memory.
