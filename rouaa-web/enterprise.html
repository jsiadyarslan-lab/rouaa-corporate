The Enterprise Problem

Institutions don't buy more information.
They buy defensible decisions.

CFOs and CIOs evaluating intelligence platforms are not asking "do you have AI?" — they are asking what changes inside their institution after deployment.

Fragmented Research Workflows

Analysts duplicate effort across disconnected systems. Source monitoring, evidence extraction, and committee preparation happen in parallel silos — with no shared evidence layer.

Compliance Reconstruction Cost

When regulators or auditors ask "where did this decision come from?" — institutions spend weeks reconstructing evidence trails that should have existed at decision time.

Slow Decision Cycles

Committees wait for research. Research waits for verification. Verification waits for source access. By the time a decision arrives, the window has moved.

Deployment Models

Choose the model that fits your institution.

Same architecture. Same trust layer. Different deployment paths — based on your governance, integration, and control requirements.

Model 01

Platform Access

Access ROUAA intelligence through the Trading Intelligence or Institutional Intelligence platforms. Fastest path to value — your teams engage with ROUAA-hosted interfaces.

Business Outcome

Replace fragmented research workflows with one governed intelligence layer. Reduce manual preparation and duplicate analyst effort.

Time To Value

Initial users onboarded: 1–2 weeks
Workflow customization: 2–4 weeks

Best For

Investment firms, banks, research houses, trading desks that want immediate access without infrastructure investment.

Model 02

API Integration

Integrate evidence, events, and intelligence directly into your existing systems. Build ROUAA's verified intelligence into your proprietary workflows, dashboards, and decision environments.

Business Outcome

Embed evidence-backed intelligence into your products and decision systems. Reduce time-to-decision and add provenance your existing stack lacks.

Time To Value

Integration: 4–12 weeks
Depending on workflow complexity

Best For

Platforms, brokers, data providers, and institutions with existing infrastructure that want ROUAA intelligence embedded in their products.

Model 03

Private Deployment

Deploy the full ROUAA architecture inside your infrastructure. Maximum control, maximum governance, maximum defensibility. Your data, your sources, your audit trails — entirely yours.

Business Outcome

Own the intelligence stack end-to-end. Reduce compliance reconstruction time and external dependencies for material decisions.

Time To Value

Security review → Infrastructure setup → Validation → Production
Implemented with ROUAA engineering

Best For

Banks, sovereign funds, and institutions with strict data residency, compliance, or sovereignty requirements.
