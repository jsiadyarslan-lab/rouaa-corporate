Solution 01 — Trading Desks

Event-Driven Market Understanding

Goal: explain why a market moved. Example: "Why did oil move?" — for traders who need the cause, not just the chart.

For trading desks that need to understand why — not just where.
Problem
Markets move before research teams understand the reason. By the time the cause is identified, the decision window has closed. Traders are left trading symptoms, not causes.
ROUAA Approach
Creates a verified causal chain: Market movement → Event → Source document → Extracted fact → Decision context — in real time, with provenance. Powered by ROUAA Evidence Infrastructure.
Outcome
Analysts understand market moves with evidence, not assumptions — before the next move happens. Traders execute on scenarios they can articulate, not on indicators they can't defend.
Solution 02 — Investment Firms

Defensible Investment Decisions

For CIO offices and investment teams — portfolio managers, research, and investment committees that reject analysis without provenance.

For investment committees that reject analysis without provenance.
Problem
Investment committees reject analysis without provenance. Decisions made on intuition don't survive governance review. Research notes that say "we believe" lose to notes that say "the document shows."
ROUAA Approach
Every fact in every research note links back to its source document, page, and paragraph — through ROUAA's evidence infrastructure. Governance rules, validation, and confidence scoring operate before analysis reaches committee.
Outcome
Investment decisions arrive at committee with full evidence chains — governance-ready, audit-ready, defensible. The conversation shifts from "is this right?" to "what do we do about what the documents show?"
Solution 03 — Quant & Trading Teams

Scenario-Aware Trading Decisions

Goal: generate governed scenarios. Example: "If scenario X materializes, what is the impact on these assets?" — for systematic teams that need defensible scenario construction.

For trading teams that need scenarios, not signals.
Problem
Traders see charts and indicators. They don't see the scenario the market is pricing in — or the scenario that's about to unfold. Indicators describe the present; they don't construct possible futures.
ROUAA Approach
Constructs governed scenarios, not just signals. Each scenario includes assumptions, supporting evidence, and risk factors — drawn from ROUAA's event intelligence, document intelligence, and knowledge graph. Powered by ROUAA's governed intelligence engine.
Outcome
Traders execute on scenarios they can articulate — not on indicators they can't defend. Each scenario carries the evidence chain required to justify the position to risk, compliance, and the trading book.
Solution 04 — Information Providers

Verifiable Financial Publishing

For financial media, data platforms, and research publishers that must publish faster without sacrificing accuracy.

For newsrooms that must publish faster without sacrificing accuracy.
Problem
Media organizations must publish faster without sacrificing accuracy. Every claim must be verifiable. Every source must be trusted. News flows faster than editorial teams can verify.
ROUAA Approach
Produces verified intelligence ready for editorial review and publication. Every claim in every published article links back to its source document, page, and paragraph — powered by ROUAA's source registry and evidence infrastructure.

Solution 05 — Banks & Financial Institutions

Governance-Ready Decision Audit

For banks that need decisions to survive regulatory review.
Problem
Banks operate under regulatory scrutiny that requires every material decision to be reconstructable. Decisions made on aggregated feeds or analyst opinions fail audit. The cost of indefensible decisions is regulatory, reputational, and financial.
ROUAA Approach
Produces decisions that are reconstructable from source to outcome. Source hierarchy, confidence scoring, and validation rules operate before analysis reaches decision-makers — powered by ROUAA's governance, evidence, and audit-ready output infrastructure.
Outcome
Every material decision — credit, market, risk, compliance — arrives with a reconstructable evidence trail. Governance-ready. Audit-ready. Defensible in front of regulators, internal audit, and the board.
