The Institutional Problem

Financial information is abundant.
Verified intelligence is not.

Fragmented Intelligence

Research, data, documents, and internal knowledge exist across disconnected systems. Institutions spend time connecting information instead of producing intelligence.

Verification Gap

Institutional intelligence requires more than information. Claims need authoritative sources, evidence, provenance, and governance that can be reconstructed when decisions are reviewed.

Speed vs Trust

News flows faster than analysis can keep up. Institutions need speed without sacrificing accuracy — and without sacrificing defensibility.
