The Problem

Official financial information is fragmented across sources, tools, and teams.

Fragmented Evidence

Evidence disconnected from decisions.

Official information lives across hundreds of sources. By the time it reaches a decision, the link to the source document is lost.

Disconnected Workflows

Each team rebuilds the same pipeline.

Research, risk, media, and trading teams each rebuild source collection, document processing, and evidence tracking from scratch.

Difficult to Defend

Decisions without traceable evidence.

When regulators or committees ask “where did this come from?”, the answer requires reconstruction projects, not a click.

The ROUA Answer

One governed intelligence foundation.

ROUA builds the intelligence infrastructure once — source governance, evidence chains, provenance, and governance metadata — and distributes governed intelligence to every workflow.
