Why institutions build intelligence layers,
not just data stacks.

Every investment firm, bank, and trading desk already owns data infrastructure. The challenge is transforming fragmented information into governed intelligence workflows.

The Data Problem

Information arrives faster than research teams can read it. Sources multiply. Confidence erodes.

The Trust Problem

Analysis without evidence doesn't survive investment committee. Decisions without provenance don't survive audit.

The Workflow Problem

Charts show where price moved. Reports explain later. Neither explains the decision in real time, with evidence.

The Intelligence Gap

Data tells institutions what happened. Research explains what it means. Few systems connect evidence, context, and decisions in one governed workflow.

ROUAA exists because the gap between information and defensible decisions creates operational risk, slower decisions, and governance exposure.

What ROUAA Is

ROUAA sits between raw data
and the decisions institutions make.

Data without evidence is noise. Decisions without provenance are exposure. ROUAA is the layer that turns one into the other — defensibly.
