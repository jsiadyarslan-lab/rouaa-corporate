Build vs Integrate

Build the evidence layer yourself — or integrate one.

The real question is not ROUA vs another API. It is: build the source governance, evidence layer, provenance, and audit trail yourself — or integrate a platform that already operates it.

Build Yourself
  • Source governance and registry infrastructure
  • Document intelligence and fact extraction pipeline
  • Evidence chain construction and provenance tracking
  • Verification tiering and source trust classification
  • Governance metadata and audit trail architecture
  • Knowledge graph and entity relationship mapping
  • Reasoning layer with auditable analytical trace
  • Ongoing source monitoring and update propagation

Sustained engineering investment before the first intelligence product ships.

Integrate ROUA
  • Embed verified financial facts and events
  • Carry evidence and provenance into downstream products
  • Build research, media, risk, and market workflows on governed intelligence
  • Preserve the distinction between source evidence and generated context
  • Integrate intelligence into existing institutional workflows
  • Inherit governance metadata without operating the governance layer
  • Deliver defensible intelligence outputs to end-users
  • Focus engineering on product experience, not infrastructure

Your team builds the product. ROUA provides the intelligence foundation underneath.

Defensible Outputs
