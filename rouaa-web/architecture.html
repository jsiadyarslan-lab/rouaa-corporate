Design Principles

What makes the architecture institutional.

Source-First, Not Data-First

Every fact begins with an identified, monitored official source — not a scraped feed. Provenance is structural, not appended.

Governance Before Output

Validation rules, confidence scoring, and source hierarchy operate before analysis is produced — not as an afterthought.

Audit-Ready By Construction

Every output is reconstructable. Source → document → fact → event → scenario → decision — fully traceable, defensible.

Intelligence Pipeline — The 7 Layers

How information flows through that architecture.

The intelligence pipeline transforms raw official documents into governed institutional intelligence — layer by layer.

01
Official Source Registry
411+ verified institutional sources including central banks, regulators, exchanges, statistical agencies, and corporate disclosures. Every source has a type, jurisdiction, update frequency, and trust tier.
↓
02
Document Intelligence
Raw documents extracted, classified, and processed into structured knowledge. PDFs, filings, press releases, and statistical releases normalized into a single queryable intelligence layer.
↓
03
Financial Facts & Events
Financial facts and market events extracted with confidence scores and source linkage. Each fact carries the document it came from, the page, the paragraph, and the extraction confidence.
↓
04
Evidence & Provenance Layer
Every fact connected to its source document, page, and paragraph. Full provenance chain. The trust layer that makes every downstream decision defensible in front of an investment committee or regulator.
↓
05
Intelligence Governance
Validation rules, confidence scoring, source hierarchy, and audit controls — operating before any analysis is produced. Governance is structural, not bolted on at the end.
↓
06
Governed Intelligence Engine
Specialized AI agents perform event analysis, macro interpretation, company analysis, risk assessment, report generation, and verification. All outputs remain constrained by verified evidence and governance rules — never producing analysis from unverified inputs. Each output carries a reasoning trace linking back to the facts and evidence it used.

Institutional Integration

ROUAA does not require institutions to replace existing systems.

It integrates with existing research platforms, data environments, workflows, and governance processes.

Governance Model

How intelligence quality is controlled.

Institutions need to know: who monitors quality? How is hallucination prevented? How are reports approved? The architecture answers these structurally.

Source Validation

Every source tier-ranked. Tier 1 official sources carry full confidence. Lower tiers require corroboration. No unverified source enters the evidence chain.

Confidence Thresholds

Facts below configurable confidence thresholds do not enter intelligence production. Institutions set their own thresholds per workflow.

Human Review Workflows

Intelligence drafts route to editorial or committee review before publication. ROUAA handles preparation — your team handles judgment.

Model Output Verification

AI outputs carry reasoning traces. Every claim links to verified evidence. Unsupported claims are structurally prevented from entering governed outputs.

Audit Logs

Every step logged — from source detection to intelligence publication. No silent operations. No undocumented transformations. Reconstructable on demand.

Access Controls

Role-based access to sources, intelligence, and workflows. Institution-defined permissions. API keys scoped per integration.
