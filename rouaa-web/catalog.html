Capability Maturity Model

Every capability is classified by operational readiness.

Not all capabilities are at the same stage. This model helps institutions understand what is production-ready today and what is under institutional validation.

Production

Operationally validated — deployed in production, serving institutional workloads, with full evidence chain and audit trail. Availability indicates deployment readiness, not just technical maturity.

Enterprise

Private deployment with institutional controls — available for regulated institutions requiring dedicated capacity, data sovereignty, or air-gapped environments.

Research Preview

Under controlled evaluation — capability is built and tested but not yet deployed at scale. Available for pilot deployments with early-access partners.
