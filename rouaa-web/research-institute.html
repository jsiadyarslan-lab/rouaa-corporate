ROUAA Research Institute

Advancing the science of
evidence-backed financial intelligence.

The ROUAA Research Institute operates as an independent research function focused on advancing evidence-backed financial intelligence. Its methodology, frameworks, and thought leadership are published openly for institutional review, critique, and adoption. Trust is built by transparency — and transparency requires publishing how the system actually works.

Institute Mission

Why a research institute inside an infrastructure company.

Building trust-layer infrastructure for institutional finance requires solving complex problems where existing approaches are incomplete: How do you score source confidence? How do you govern AI on unverified inputs? How do you construct an evidence chain that survives regulatory review? The Institute exists to solve these problems — and to publish the solutions.

Open Methodology

We publish how ROUAA works — source hierarchy, confidence scoring, evidence chain construction, governance rules. Institutions can verify, critique, and adopt what we publish.

Frameworks

Reference frameworks for evidence-backed decision workflows — usable by institutions whether or not they deploy ROUAA. Trust infrastructure benefits everyone.

Research Partnerships

Collaborations with academic institutions, think tanks, and research houses on the open problems of institutional financial intelligence.

Current Research Program

What the Institute is working on now.

Research programs already in progress — not future promises. Each addresses a specific open problem in evidence-backed financial intelligence, and each will be published for institutional review.

01
Evidence Chain Specification v1.0
A framework defining how financial claims move from source documents to institutional decisions — provenance structure, paragraph-level linking, confidence propagation, and the structural properties of defensible evidence.
02
Source Trust Model v1.0
A methodology for classifying and scoring financial information sources — trust tiers, jurisdictional coverage, monitoring methodology, and the lifecycle of sources from addition to retirement.
03
Governed AI Framework v1.0
Architectural principles for AI systems operating on verified evidence — reasoning traces, confidence propagation, validation-before-output, and the constraints that make AI outputs audit-ready.

Additional programs are in earlier stages — including knowledge graph ontology design, cross-jurisdictional evidence recognition, and the economics of institutional trust. These will be added to the public program as they mature.

Research Areas

Five areas of active research.

Each area addresses an open problem in the construction of defensible institutional intelligence.

Area 01

Source Verification

How do you establish source trust at scale? Source hierarchy design, jurisdictional coverage, monitoring methodology, and the lifecycle of sources — addition, deprecation, retirement.

Area 02

Evidence Architecture

How do you construct evidence chains that survive audit? Provenance design, fact extraction confidence, paragraph-level linking, and the structural properties of defensible evidence.

Area 03

Governance Models

How do you govern intelligence production before output? Validation rules, confidence scoring, source hierarchy, and audit controls — designed as architecture, not policy.

Area 04

Knowledge Graphs

How do you represent financial reality as a queryable graph? Entity resolution, event extraction, fact linkage, and institutional-grade ontology design.

Area 05

Governed AI

How do you produce AI intelligence that is defensible? Operating AI on verified evidence only, reasoning traces, confidence propagation, and the architectural constraints that make AI outputs audit-ready.

Open

Emerging Areas

Cross-jurisdictional evidence recognition, regulatory alignment frameworks, and the economics of institutional trust — open research questions we are exploring with partners.
