ROUA Research Institute

The research capability behind
ROUA intelligence products.

The ROUA Research Institute is not an independent research firm. It is the internal research function that builds the methodology, frameworks, and evidence architecture powering ROUA's intelligence products. Selected research, methodologies, and frameworks are published for institutional review — because transparency is essential to earning trust in intelligence infrastructure.

Institute Mission

Why a research capability inside an intelligence company.

ROUA delivers intelligence products across Investment, Market, Risk, Media, and Trading. Each product is only as defensible as the evidence and methodology beneath it. The Institute exists to solve the open problems those products depend on: How do you score source confidence? How do you govern AI on ungoverned inputs? How do you construct evidence chains that can withstand institutional, audit, and regulatory scrutiny? Research answers these questions; the products inherit the answers.

Current Research Program

What the Institute is working on now.

Current and developing research programs. Each addresses a specific open problem in evidence-backed financial intelligence, and findings are shared through institutional review.

01
Evidence Chain Specification v1.0
A framework defining how financial claims move from source documents to institutional decisions — provenance structure, paragraph-level linking, confidence propagation, and the structural properties of defensible evidence.
02
Source Trust Model v1.0
A methodology for classifying and scoring financial information sources — trust tiers, jurisdictional coverage, monitoring methodology, and the lifecycle of sources from addition to retirement.
03
Governed AI Framework v1.0
Architectural principles for AI systems operating on verified evidence — auditable analytical traces, confidence propagation, validation-before-output, and the constraints that make AI outputs evidence-linked.

Additional programs are in earlier stages — including knowledge graph ontology design, cross-jurisdictional evidence recognition, and the economics of institutional trust. These will be added to the public program as they mature.

Research Areas

Research areas shaping ROUA's intelligence foundation.

Each area addresses an open problem in evidence-backed institutional intelligence. Research findings may inform ROUA's methodologies, architecture, governance, and product development.

Area 01

Source Verification

How do you establish source trust at scale? Source hierarchy design, jurisdictional coverage, monitoring methodology, and the lifecycle of sources — addition, deprecation, retirement.

Area 02

Evidence Architecture

How do you construct evidence chains that survive audit? Provenance design, fact extraction confidence, paragraph-level linking, and the structural properties of defensible evidence.

Area 03

Governance Models

How do you govern intelligence production before output? Validation rules, confidence scoring, source hierarchy, and audit controls — designed as architecture, not policy.

Area 04

Knowledge Graphs

How do you represent financial reality as a queryable graph? Entity resolution, event extraction, fact linkage, and institutional-grade ontology design.

Area 05

Governed AI

How do you produce AI intelligence that is defensible? Operating AI on governed evidence and constrained inputs, auditable analytical traces, confidence propagation, and the architectural constraints that make AI outputs evidence-linked.

Open

Emerging Areas

Cross-jurisdictional evidence recognition, regulatory alignment frameworks, and the economics of institutional trust — open research questions we are exploring with partners.
