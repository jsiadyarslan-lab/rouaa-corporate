The Risk Problem

Risk decisions deserve evidence.
Most arrive with none.

Risk teams and compliance officers govern the institution's exposure to regulatory, geopolitical, and operational events. Yet the intelligence feeding those decisions is too often built on assertion, not evidence.

No Traceability

Risk teams lack traceability.

Risk assessments summarize, flag, and escalate — but rarely link exposures back to the official source, regulatory clause, or geopolitical event that established them. Risk committees are asked to act on conclusions they cannot verify.

Slow Responses

Regulatory responses take days.

When regulators ask how exposure was assessed, the answer requires days of reconstruction — pulling emails, spreadsheets, and analyst notes across fragmented systems. Time spent assembling evidence is time the institution is exposed.

Latent Exposure

Exposure unknown until too late.

Sanctions, regulatory changes, and geopolitical events propagate through counterparties, holdings, and jurisdictions faster than manual monitoring can track. By the time exposure is mapped, the institution is already positioned in it.

Deployment Models

Deploy where your governance requires.

Three deployment models — same evidence architecture, same governance controls. Regulated institutions typically require private or on-premise deployment to meet supervisory expectations on data sovereignty and audit isolation.

Deployment 01

Private Cloud

Dedicated, isolated ROUAA environment within your cloud tenancy — evidence layer, knowledge graph, and governance controls operated under your security perimeter with full tenant isolation.

Ideal For
Regulated institutions requiring tenant isolation, dedicated capacity, and cloud-native controls without sacrificing data sovereignty.
Deployment 02

Hybrid

Source ingestion and evidence extraction in a controlled cloud environment, with sensitive exposure mapping and evidence packs retained inside your perimeter. Governance controls span both sides.

Ideal For
Institutions balancing scale of source monitoring against residency constraints on counterparty and exposure data.
Deployment 03

On-Premise

Full ROUAA intelligence infrastructure deployed inside your data center — complete data sovereignty, evidence provenance, and governance under your direct control, including air-gapped operation.

Ideal For
Regulated institutions with strict data residency, sovereignty, or air-gapped supervisory requirements — typically banks, supervisors, and systemically important institutions.
