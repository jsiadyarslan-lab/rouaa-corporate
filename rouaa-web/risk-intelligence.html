The Risk Problem

Risk decisions deserve evidence.
Most arrive with none.

Risk teams and compliance officers govern the institution's exposure to regulatory, geopolitical, and operational events. Yet the intelligence feeding those decisions is too often built on assertion, not evidence.

No Traceability

Risk teams lack traceability.

Risk assessments summarize, flag, and escalate — but often fail to preserve a direct evidence chain back to the official source, regulatory clause, or geopolitical event that established the underlying risk. Risk committees are asked to act on conclusions they cannot verify.

Latent Exposure

Exposure unknown until too late.

Sanctions, regulatory changes, and geopolitical events propagate through counterparties, portfolios, and jurisdictions faster than manual monitoring can track. By the time exposure is mapped, the institution is already positioned in it.

Slow Responses

Regulatory responses take days.

When regulators ask how exposure was assessed, the answer requires days of reconstruction — pulling emails, spreadsheets, and analyst notes across fragmented systems. Time spent assembling evidence is time the institution is exposed.

Accountability Gap

Risk decisions cannot be replayed.

When regulators or boards ask 'why did you hold this exposure?', risk teams cannot reconstruct the evidence base that existed at the time of the decision. Audit trails become reconstruction projects, not governance records.

ROUA
Replayable risk decision records — each exposure, scenario, and action preserved with the evidence that justified it, reconstructable on demand for regulators and boards.

The consequence is consistent across institutions: risk decisions become difficult to verify, difficult to audit, and difficult to defend.

How It Works

From regulatory event to audit-ready decision.

The buyer workflow — not the internal pipeline. Where ROUA enters the institution's risk operation, and what changes at each step.

01
Monitor Before exposure changes
Official regulatory and sanctions sources monitored through configured source monitoring — OFAC actions, regulator publications, central bank statements, and enforcement notices. Risk teams start with source-linked event context, not raw alerts.
↓
02
Detect When an event breaks
A material risk event arrives as a structured intelligence object — the verified event, designated entities, blocked property, and the provenance trail (source document, paragraph, exact text) preserved together. The team starts from “what does this mean for us,” not “what happened.”
↓
03
Assess Exposure Connecting event to exposure
The event is placed into institutional context — counterparties, portfolios, jurisdictions, and ownership/control relationships become one connected intelligence view. Exposure review areas surface that isolated list-checking cannot reveal. ROUA Risk Context layer is clearly separated from the verified event.
↓
04
Escalate / Review Acting with evidence
The risk decision — escalation, exposure reduction, or committee notification — is made with the evidence chain attached. Compliance evidence packs assembled on demand: sources, extracted clauses, exposure mappings, and analyst review preserved together. Each action carries the evidence package.
↓
05
Decision Record Audit & governance
Weeks or months later, the risk assessment is reconstructable — the event, the evidence, the exposure review, the escalation, and the decision context that justified the action at the time. Regulators and boards see replayable governance records, not reconstruction projects.
