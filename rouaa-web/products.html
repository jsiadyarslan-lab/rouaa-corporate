What Institutions Build With ROUAA

You're not buying software. You're building institutional capability.

Source Registry

411+ verified institutional sources across central banks, regulators, exchanges, statistical agencies, and corporate disclosures. Monitored, tier-ranked, and governed.

Evidence Graph

Every fact linked to its source document, page, and paragraph. Every analysis traceable to verified evidence. Every output reconstructable on demand.

Knowledge Graph

Entities, events, facts, and relationships connected through an institutional-grade ontology. Builds incrementally — never resets.

Decision Memory

Intelligence Methodology

Institutional reasoning frameworks, validation processes, and decision workflows accumulated over time. Not just data — the methodology that turns data into defensible intelligence.
