Foundational Principle

ROUA does not optimize for producing more outputs.
It optimizes for producing defensible outputs.

The walkthrough below uses an FOMC statement as an illustrative example of how ROUA transforms an official publication into a governed, evidence-linked Intelligence Object. The intended workflow records the transformation path, links claims to evidence, and preserves the information required for audit and review. This is the path from official publication to institutional decision.

The Institutional Problem

Most intelligence systems answer:

“What happened?”

What Institutions Need

Institutions need to answer:

“Can we prove why we believe it?”

The Evidence Explorer answers the second question — by showing the chain.

What You Can Prove With ROUA

Every step answers an institutional question.

The evidence chain is not a technical pipeline. It is the set of questions your audit committee, compliance team, and regulators will ask — and that ROUA lets you answer on demand.

Source?

Which official source produced this. Identity, endpoint, and jurisdiction — not “a feed.”

What changed?

The specific fact extracted — linked to document, page, and paragraph. Not a summary.

What evidence supports it?

The provenance chain — fact → document → location → source. Click-through on demand.

Who reviewed it?

Governance validation + authorized analyst sign-off. Not auto-published silently.

Can I reproduce it?

Yes. The full chain is sealed in the audit trail. Reconstructable six months later — on demand.

How confident is it?

A composite confidence score — source tier, extraction, corroboration, recency. Published with the fact.

Human Accountability Layer

The system produces evidence. The institution owns the decision.

Human review is a real gate, not a marketing claim. Between Step 06 (Object creation) and Step 07 (Publication), an authorized analyst reviews the evidence, confirms the confidence signal, and signs off. The reviewer’s identity is recorded in the object’s provenance — permanently. The system can process. Only institutions can approve. For lower-stakes objects, review thresholds may differ — but the review state is always recorded.
