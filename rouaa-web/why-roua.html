Why Existing Platforms Cannot Simply Add This

Trust cannot be added after intelligence is generated.

A natural question from an institution that already has data platforms and terminals: "Why can't they just add this layer?" The answer is structural — not a feature gap, but an architecture gap.

The Required Direction of Trust
Source → Document → Fact → Evidence → Reasoning → Decision

Information platforms are built to deliver information to screens — then the user decides what to do with it. Evidence infrastructure is built in the opposite direction: trust is established at the source layer and preserved upward through documents, facts, evidence, reasoning, and finally the decision.

Adding evidence and governance to an existing terminal means retrofitting the entire chain — re-ingesting sources with provenance, rebuilding the document layer, populating a knowledge graph, and establishing governance before output. That is not a feature addition. It is a rebuild of how the platform works at the data layer.

Why Institutions Need This Now

The conditions that made ROUA necessary did not exist five years ago.

Information Volume

The volume of official financial information — central bank communications, regulatory filings, statistical releases — has grown beyond what manual research workflows can keep up with. Source monitoring is no longer a headcount problem.

AI Acceleration, Governance Gap

General AI accelerates analysis — but without governance, it increases institutional risk. Hallucinated claims and unprovenanced outputs are a regulatory liability, not a productivity gain. The missing layer is governed intelligence infrastructure.

Regulator Expectations

Regulators increasingly require decision traceability — not only that the right decision was made, but that the institution can show how it was made. Decisions built on aggregated feeds or analyst opinions fail this test.

Documented Decision Reasoning

Investment committees, risk committees, and audit committees now expect decisions to carry documented reasoning — not intuition. The infrastructure to produce that reasoning on demand did not previously exist as a packaged layer.

The missing layer is not more data. It is trusted intelligence infrastructure — connecting verified information to defensible decisions under governance.
